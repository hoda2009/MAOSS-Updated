Cultural Achievement

Students shine at the LIS Cultural Week

Congratulations to Farah Al-Fleiti for earning first place in the short story competition, and Sara Al-Denef for earning third place in poetry during Cultural Week at LIS.

We are proud of their creativity and achievements. Special thanks to the High School Arabic Language Committee, especially coordinator Ms. Samar Bahmad, for supporting our students.

06
STEAM & Innovation

STEAM students showcase “Print Me a Bottle”

MAOSS High School STEAM Lab students participated in the Industrialists and Food Exhibition at Forum de Beyrouth, presenting their “Print Me a Bottle” project and showcasing creativity, technical skills, and hands-on innovation.

Special thanks to teachers Layal Maalawi and Imad Malaab for their guidance and support.
