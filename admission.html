Tuition & Fees

Tuition Fees

Tuition fees are determined in accordance with the official regulations and educational policies approved by the Ministry of Education for the accredited curricula. The school is committed to maintaining transparency and fairness in all financial matters while continuing to provide high-quality education and distinguished academic services to students.

Important Information

Tuition fees are set based on the national educational system and curriculum requirements.

Fees may vary depending on the academic level and grade.

Payment schedules and procedures are announced by the school administration.

Additional fees for activities, books or services may apply when necessary.
