School Leadership

Dr. Sanaa Shehayeb

Headmistress

Dr. Shehayeb leads MAOSS with a vision centered on academic excellence, character development, and meaningful partnership with the school community. Her leadership reflects the school's commitment to creating an environment where students can grow in knowledge, confidence, and purpose.

Mr. Khaled Abd El Samad

Founding Principal · 1969–1975
