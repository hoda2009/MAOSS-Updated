Credentials

🎓 Qualifications

We are committed to maintaining high academic standards by ensuring that all students achieve recognized qualifications that prepare them for higher education and future careers.

📖

Humanities

Literary Track Qualification

Students in the Literary track receive qualifications focused on humanities and social sciences:

  • 📚 Languages, Literature, Philosophy, Sociology
  • 🌍 Preparation for education, law, and humanities fields
  • 🧠 Development of communication and analytical skills
🔬

STEM

Scientific Track Qualification

Students in the Scientific track earn qualifications focused on science and mathematics:

  • 🧪 Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics
  • 💻 Preparation for medical, engineering, and scientific fields
  • 🎯 Strong foundation for university STEM programs
📚

Graduation

High School Certificate

Students who successfully complete the secondary level receive an official High School Certificate, which confirms their academic achievement and readiness for university studies.

  • 🎓 Recognized national secondary certificate
  • 📊 Based on continuous assessment and final examinations
  • 🏫 Required for university admission

🏆 Skill Development Certificates

In addition to academic qualifications, students may receive certificates for:

💻

Computer skills and digital literacy

🗣️

Language proficiency (Arabic, English, French, Italian)

🏅

Participation in academic and extracurricular activities

📌 Our Goal

Behind every successful student is a dedicated teacher. At MAOSS, our staff is not just a team — we are a family united by a shared purpose: to inspire, educate, and empower the next generation.
