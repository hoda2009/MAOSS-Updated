Parents & Guardians

Building a strong partnership between home and school to support every student's academic journey and personal growth.

Partnership

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Communication with School

At Maroun Abboud Secondary School (MASS) — Aley, we believe that strong communication between parents and the school is essential for student success and development.

Contact

📞 Direct Communication

Parents can contact the school administration and teachers for any academic or behavioral concerns:

📱

Phone calls during school working hours

🏫

Scheduled meetings with teachers or administration

🗂️

Office visits for official matters

Updates

📢 School Announcements

Important updates are regularly shared with parents through:

📄

Official notices sent via students

📱

WhatsApp / SMS notifications (if available)

🏫

School bulletin boards

🌐

School website updates

Meetings

👨‍🏫 Parent-Teacher Meetings

We organize regular meetings to discuss student progress:

📊

Academic performance reviews

🧠

Behavior and discipline discussions

🎯

Improvement plans and recommendations

Involvement

🤝 Collaboration with Parents

We encourage parents to actively participate in their child's education by:

📚

Monitoring homework and study time

🏠

Supporting a healthy learning environment at home

🧑‍🏫

Communicating regularly with teachers

🎓

Attending school events and meetings

Mission

📌 Our Goal

Our goal is to build a strong partnership between parents and the school to ensure every student achieves their full potential in a supportive and motivating environment.

Rules

📜 Guidelines & Policies

Our guidelines and policies are designed to maintain a safe, respectful, and productive learning environment for all students and staff.

Behavior

🏫 School Discipline

We expect all students to follow school rules and show proper behavior:

🧑‍🎓

Respect teachers, staff, and classmates

🏫

Maintain discipline inside and outside classrooms

📵

Avoid disruptive or inappropriate behavior

🎯

Follow instructions from school administration

Attendance

⏰ Attendance & Punctuality

Regular attendance is essential for academic success:

📅

Students must attend school daily

⏰

Arrive on time for all classes

📝

Absences should be justified by parents or guardians

📢

Repeated absenteeism may affect academic performance

Honesty

📚 Academic Integrity

We promote honesty and fairness in all academic work:

🚫

No cheating during exams or quizzes

📖

Submit original homework and assignments

🧠

Respect intellectual effort and learning ethics

Conduct

📱 School Conduct

Students are expected to follow proper behavior in all school environments:

📵

Limited use of mobile phones during school hours

🤝

Respect school property and facilities

🏫

Maintain cleanliness in classrooms and common areas

Safety

🛡️ Safety & Well-being

The school prioritizes student safety and well-being:

🧑‍🏫

Follow safety instructions at all times

🚪

No leaving school premises without permission

🤝

Report any issues to school staff immediately
