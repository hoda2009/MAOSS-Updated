Students at MAOSS

Empowering every student to achieve academic excellence through structured learning, fair assessment, and continuous support.

Academic Expectations

📝 Homework & Exams

At Maroun Abboud Secondary School (MASS) — Aley, homework and examinations are an essential part of the learning process, helping students strengthen their understanding and achieve academic excellence.

Policy

📚 Homework Policy

Homework is assigned to reinforce classroom learning and develop independent study habits.

📖

Daily assignments are given according to each subject

🧠

Homework helps students review and practice lessons

⏰

Students are expected to submit work on time

🤝

Parents are encouraged to support study routines at home

Assessment

📝 Exam System

Our examination system is designed to evaluate student progress fairly and effectively:

📊

Daily quizzes to check understanding

📅

Monthly tests for continuous assessment

🎓

Mid-term exams to evaluate semester progress

🏆

Final exams for overall academic performance

Rules

📌 Important Guidelines

🚫

No cheating or unfair practices are allowed

📵

Students must follow exam rules and instructions

⏳

Punctuality is required for all exams

🎒

Proper preparation is strongly encouraged

Performance

🎓 Student Results / Grades

Student results and grades are an important part of tracking academic progress and supporting continuous improvement throughout the school year.

Evaluation

📊 Grading System

Students are evaluated based on a clear and fair grading system:

📝

Class participation and daily performance

📚

Homework and assignments

📊

Quizzes and monthly tests

🎓

Mid-term and final examinations

Scale

🏆 Result Evaluation

Each student's performance is assessed to ensure academic growth:

🌟
Excellent (A)

Outstanding performance

👍
Very Good (B)

Strong understanding of subjects

📖
Good (C)

Satisfactory performance

⚠️
Needs Improvement (D)

Additional support required

Timeline

📅 Result Announcements

📢

Results are announced at the end of each term

🧑‍🏫

Parents are informed through official school communication

📄

Report cards are provided for detailed performance review

Support

🧠 Student Improvement

We support students to improve their grades through:

📚

Extra revision classes

👨‍🏫

Teacher feedback and guidance

📝

Practice exercises and mock exams

🤝

Academic counseling when needed

Final Word

📌 Important Note

Students are encouraged to focus on continuous learning rather than only final grades, as improvement and effort are highly valued.
