🪑Quiet study area for students
💻Support for research and assignments

📖Storybooks and novels
📚Academic textbooks and guides
🌍Encyclopedias and reference books

Health & Safety
🏥

Medical Clinic

The school medical clinic is dedicated to ensuring the health, safety, and well-being of all students and staff during the school day.

🧑‍⚕️ About the Clinic

Our clinic provides basic first aid and immediate medical attention for minor health issues and emergencies that may occur during school hours.

🏥On-site medical support
🧑‍⚕️First aid assistance
🩹Care for minor injuries or illness
📞Communication with parents in case of emergencies
💊 Services Provided
🩹Wound cleaning and bandaging
🌡️Fever and basic health checks
🤕Headache and minor pain management
🧠Monitoring student health conditions
📋 Health & Safety Monitoring
🧑‍⚕️Regular health supervision
📊Tracking student health reports (if required)
🚨Emergency response procedures
🤝Coordination with parents and medical professionals
📌
Important Note: Parents are contacted immediately in case of any serious medical situation, and students are referred to professional healthcare services when necessary.

🎯 Our Goal

To ensure a safe, healthy, and supportive environment so that students can focus on learning with peace of mind.
