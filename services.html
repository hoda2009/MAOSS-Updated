School Services

Maroun Abboud Public High School (MAOSS) — Aley provides a comprehensive range of services designed to support students' academic development, health, and personal growth beyond the classroom.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Support for Every Student

From academic resources to health care and extracurricular development, MAOSS is committed to supporting the whole student.

Library Medical Clinic Sports & Activities

School Library

Medical Clinic

Sports & Activities

Learning Resource Center

School Library

The MAOSS school library is a key learning space that supports students' academic growth, reading culture, and independent learning.

About the Library

Our library provides a quiet and comfortable environment where students can study, read, and explore a wide range of educational resources.

Rich collection of books and references
Academic resources for all subjects
Quiet study area for students
Support for research and assignments
Available Resources
Storybooks and novels
Academic textbooks and guides
Encyclopedias and reference books
Digital learning materials, where available
Library Services
Book borrowing and return system
Research assistance for students
Study support for projects and assignments
Reading sessions and activities

Our Goal

To foster a love for reading and provide every student with the resources they need to excel academically.

Health & Safety

Medical Clinic

The school medical clinic supports the health, safety, and well-being of students and staff throughout the school day.

About the Clinic

The clinic provides basic first aid and immediate attention for minor health concerns and situations that may occur during school hours.

On-site medical support
First aid assistance
Care for minor injuries or illness
Communication with parents when necessary
Services Provided
Wound cleaning and bandaging
Basic health checks
Attention for minor health concerns
Monitoring student well-being
Health & Safety Monitoring
Regular health supervision
Health documentation where required
Emergency response procedures
Coordination with parents when necessary
Important Note: Parents are contacted in serious medical situations, and students are referred to professional healthcare services when necessary.

Our Goal

To maintain a safe, healthy, and supportive environment so students can focus on learning with peace of mind.
